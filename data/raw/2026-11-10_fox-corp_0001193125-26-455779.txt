Issuer: | Fox Corporation (the “ Issuer ”)
Change of Control Triggering Event: | Upon the occurrence of a Change of Control Triggering Event, other than with respect to any series of the Notes for which the Issuer has exercised its option to redeem all of the Notes of such series or has defeased or discharged
the Notes of such series as described in the Indenture, the Issuer will be required to make an offer to purchase the Notes at a purchase price equal to 101% of the principal amount of the Notes, plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, on the Notes
repurchased to, but excluding, the date of repurchase.
Optional Redemption Provisions: | Prior to the applicable Par Call Date (as defined below), the Issuer may redeem of any series of Notes at its option, in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time, at a redemption price (expressed as a percentage of
principal amount and rounded to three decimal places) equal to the greater of: (1) (a) the sum of the present values of the remaining scheduled payments of principal and interest thereon discounted to the redemption date (assuming the Notes matured
on the applicable Par Call Date) on a semi-annual basis (assuming a 360-day year consisting of twelve 30-day months) at the Treasury Rate plus: (i) 15 basis points,
in the case of the 2029 Notes, (ii) 20 basis points, in the case of the 2031 Notes, (iii) 20 basis points, in the case of the 2033 Notes, (iv) 25 basis points, in the case of the 2036 Notes, (v) 30 basis points, in the case of
the 2046 Notes, (vi) 30 basis points, in the case of the 2056 Notes, and (vii) 30 basis points, in the case of the 2066 Notes, less (b) interest accrued to the date of redemption; and (2) 100% of the principal amount of the Notes
to be redeemed; plus, in either case, accrued and unpaid interest thereon to the redemption date.

On or after the applicable Par Call Date, the Issuer may redeem the Notes of any series, in whole or in part, at any time and from time to time, at a redemption price equal to 100% of the principal amount of the Notes being redeemed
plus accrued and unpaid interest thereon to the redemption date.
“ Par Call Date ” means (i) with respect to the 2029 Notes, October 15, 2029 (the date that is one (1) month prior to the maturity date of the 2029 Notes), (ii) with respect to the 2031 Notes,
October 15, 2031 (the date that is one (1) month prior to the maturity date of the 2031 Notes), (iii) with respect to the 2033 Notes, September 15, 2033 (the date that is two (2) months prior to the maturity
date of the 2033 Notes), (iv) with respect to the 2036 Notes, August 15, 2036 (the date that is three (3) months prior to the maturity date of the 2036 Notes), (v) with respect to the 2046 Notes, May 15, 2046 (the date
that is six (6) months prior to the maturity date of the 2046 Notes), (vi) with respect to the 2056 Notes, May 15, 2056 (the date that is six (6) months prior to the maturity date of the 2056 Notes), and (vii) with
respect to the 2066 Notes, May 15, 2066 (the date that is six (6) months prior to the maturity date of the 2066 Notes).
Trade Date: | November 10, 2026
Settlement Date: | November 13, 2026 (T+2)
The Issuer expects to deliver the Notes in book-entry form through the facility of the DTC on or about November 13, 2026, which will be the second business day following the initial trade of the Notes (this settlement cycle
being referred to as “T+2”). Under Rule 15c6-1 under Exchange Act, trades in the secondary market generally are required to settle in one business day, unless the parties to any such trade
expressly agree otherwise. Accordingly, purchasers who wish to trade the Notes prior to the business day preceding the settlement date will be required, by virtue of the fact that the Notes initially will settle in T+2, to specify an alternate
settlement cycle at the time of any such trade to prevent a failed settlement. Purchasers of the Notes who wish to trade the Notes prior to the business day preceding the settlement date should consult their own
advisors.
