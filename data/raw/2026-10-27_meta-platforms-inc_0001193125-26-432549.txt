reference both the Class A Common Stock and the Class C Common Stock (as such term is defined in the Common Stock Preliminary Prospectus Supplement) and, in certain circumstances, the Issuer will have the

Initial Forward Sale Price: | $622.91875 per share, which is the Public Offering Price of the Class A Common Stock in the Common Stock Offering less the applicable underwriting discount for the Common Stock Offering. The forward sale agreements provide
that the Initial Forward Sale Price is subject to adjustment on a daily basis based on a floating interest rate factor equal to the overnight bank funding rate less a spread and will be subject to decrease on each of certain dates specified
in the forward sale agreements by amounts related to expected dividends on shares of Class A Common Stock or Class C Common Stock, as applicable, during the term of the forward sale agreements.
Upon the occurrence of a Class C Effective Date (as such term is defined in the Common Stock Preliminary Prospectus Supplement), each forward sale agreement will become two separate transactions, each with terms identical to
those of the applicable forward sale agreement immediately prior to the Class C Effective Date, except with one transaction having shares of Class A Common Stock as the underlying shares (a “Class A Transaction”) and the
other transaction having Class C Common Stock as the underlying shares and with such further changes as required under the terms of each forward sale agreement. Subject to certain conditions, the Issuer will have the ability to settle the
Class A Transactions through the delivery of shares of Class C Common Stock, with an adjustment to the forward sale price applicable to such settlement.
See “Underwriting (Conflicts of Interest)—Forward Sale Agreements” in the Common Stock Preliminary Prospectus Supplement.
Use of Proceeds: | The Issuer does not expect to initially receive any proceeds from the sale of shares of Class A Common Stock offered by the Forward Sellers in the Common Stock Offering unless an event occurs that requires the Issuer to sell
such shares to the underwriters in the Common Stock Offering in lieu of the Forward Sellers selling such shares to such underwriters (including in connection with the exercise of such underwriters’ option to purchase additional shares, in
whole or in part), in which case the Issuer intends to use all net proceeds from any such sales for the same purposes described below.
The Issuer expects to receive net proceeds, before expenses, of approximately $24.9 billion (or approximately $28.7 billion if the underwriters’ option to purchase additional shares is exercised in full), subject to
the price adjustment and other provisions of the forward sale agreements, upon settlement of the forward sale agreements, which the Issuer expects to occur on or prior to November 19, 2027.
The actual proceeds to us from the forward sales are subject to the final settlement of the forward sale agreements. The Issuer currently intends to use the net proceeds, if any, that it receives upon settlement of the forward sale
agreements for general corporate purposes.

extent that the Issuer is legally permitted to pay dividends and its board of directors, or an authorized committee thereof, declares a dividend payable with respect to the Series A Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock, the Issuer initially will
pay such dividends in cash or, subject to certain limitations, by delivery of shares of Class A Common Stock or through any combination of cash and shares of Class A Common Stock, as determined by the Issuer’s board of directors in
its sole discretion; provided that any unpaid dividends will continue to accumulate. Following the Class C Dividend Trigger Date (as defined in the Series A Depositary Shares Preliminary Prospectus Supplement), any declared dividends may
be payable in cash or, subject to certain limitations, by delivery of shares of Class C Common Stock or through any combination of cash and shares of Class C Common Stock, as determined by the Issuer’s board of directors in its sole
discretion.
