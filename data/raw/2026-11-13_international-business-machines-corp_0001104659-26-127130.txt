**Note: We expect that delivery of the Notes
will be made to investors on or about November 19, 2026, which will be the fourth business day following the date of this Pricing
Term Sheet (such settlement being referred to as “T+4”). Under Rule 15c6-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as
amended (the “Exchange Act”), trades in the secondary market generally are required to settle in one New York business day
(as such term is used for purposes of Rule 15c6-1 of the Exchange Act), unless the parties to any such trade expressly agree otherwise.
Accordingly, purchasers who wish to trade the Notes on any date prior to the first New York business day preceding the settlement date
will be required, by virtue of the fact that the Notes will initially settle in T+4, to specify an alternate settlement arrangement at
the time of any such trade to prevent a failed settlement; such purchasers should consult their own advisors in this regard.
