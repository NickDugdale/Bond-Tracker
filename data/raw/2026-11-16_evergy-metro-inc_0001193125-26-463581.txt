Make-Whole Call: | Prior to September 1, 2036 (the date that is three months prior to the maturity date of the mortgage bonds (the “Par Call Date”)), the Issuer may redeem the mortgage bonds at any time in whole, or from time to time

scheduled
payments of principal and interest thereon discounted to the redemption date (assuming the mortgage bonds matured on the Par Call Date) on a semi-annual basis (assuming a 360-day year

plus, in either case, accrued and unpaid interest thereon to, but excluding, the redemption date.
Par Call: | On or after the Par Call Date, the Issuer may redeem the mortgage bonds at any time in whole, or from time to time in part, at its option, at a redemption price equal to 100% of the principal amount of the mortgage bonds to be
redeemed, plus accrued and unpaid interest thereon to, but excluding, the redemption date.
Tax Credit Event Call: | Upon the occurrence of a Tax Credit Event, the Issuer may redeem the mortgage bonds, in whole but not in part, at its option, at a redemption price equal to 101% of the principal amount of the mortgage bonds to be redeemed, plus
accrued and unpaid interest thereon to, but excluding, the redemption date (provided that the notice of such redemption may be issued no later than May 20, 2027).
