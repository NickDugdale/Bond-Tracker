*Note:
The Issuer expects that delivery of the Notes will be made against payment therefor on or about the Settlement Date indicated above,

Under Rule 15c6-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, trades in the secondary market generally are required

wish to trade the Notes prior to their date of delivery should consult their own advisor.
